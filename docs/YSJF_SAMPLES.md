On each material page the library offers the 4K file at price 0 under three
licences: personal learning, personal commercial, and enterprise commercial.
These pages were checked on 2026-11-11. The files shipped here are the
publicly served preview stills, cropped to remove black side bars and resized
for the website. They are used as demonstration frames inside the film
preview, not resold as a stock pack.

Manufacturer award galleries were not used. Their published terms grant the
sponsor a licence; they do not grant third parties the right to republish the
photographs.
