Notes on Prototype vs. Native Engine Data
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2026-11-10 — Native Engine Profile & LUT Baking (app/ & engine/)
  - In the repository's native Android build (app/ + engine/), upstream
    spektrafilm v0.3.4 profiles (src/spektrafilm/data/profiles/*.json),
    Hanatos 2025 tc_lut tables, CAT16 matrices, scan_lut 3D LUTs, and optical
    tables were baked into SPK1 binary files (*.spk) via tools/bake/bake_spektrafilm.py.

2026-11-11 — WebView Prototype Wrapper Notices (android/)
  - This APK (app.spektralab.prototype) is the offline CSS/SVG WebView
    interaction prototype. It does NOT bundle .spk binary profile/LUT files;
    film treatments here are approximate CSS filters over built-in SVG scenes.
  - Because the repository derives from spektrafilm and documents both the
    interactive prototype and the native engine, the full unmodified
    SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt (CC BY-SA 4.0 + preamble) and this CHANGELOG.txt
    are bundled locally alongside GPL-3.0.txt and NOTICE.txt.
