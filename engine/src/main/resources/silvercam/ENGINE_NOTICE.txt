LibRaw-derived RAW decoding and camera matrices (2026-11-12)

CameraMatrices.kt contains 808 camera coefficient rows, maker normalization and
model aliases generated by tools/gen_camera_matrices.py from LibRaw 0.22.2,
release tag 0.22.2, commit b93f6e45c194f5df9b02a43b1af9a54b4f41f33f:
https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw/tree/b93f6e45c194f5df9b02a43b1af9a54b4f41f33f
Inputs: src/tables/colordata.cpp, src/metadata/identify.cpp,
src/metadata/normalize_model.cpp and libraw/libraw_version.h. Regeneration from
these pinned files was byte-identical to the imported Kotlin table. Exact input
SHA-256 hashes are recorded in tools/libraw-provenance.json.
